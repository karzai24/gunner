## Concrete implementation gates

1. **Crouch entry/exit:** explicit presentation phases now follow actual native stance admission and support rapid reversal and cancellation by movement/aim/actions. Low cover bypasses transition weight immediately. The recovered 30-scenario suite and inspected captures now validate the bounded rifle/free and pistol/high-cover stance/carry fixtures plus movement/ADS interruption and low-cover bypass. Rapid reversals and every action-order permutation are not certified by those fixtures.
2. **High-cover rifle entry/exit:** select a source phase and playback duration that fit the accepted route. Align the inward approach, authored turn and outward idle explicitly; the current actor-facing interpolation must not add a second 180° turn. Retain swept movement, current-wall/floor checks, cancellation and combat guards. Measure weapon grip during the transition, including the graph's existing 25 cm wall inset. Start with the proven entry/right-exit family before fitting other sides.
3. **Transfer or corner:** validate destination wall normal, height, support, complete swept route and cancellation before committing an anchor. Treat the crouched transfer as exposed during transit. A side-switch clip alone does not justify assigning a new wall normal or enabling corner traversal.
4. **Vault:** sample every source frame, adding `ball_l/r`, `calf_l/r`, `index_metacarpal_l/r` and the existing root/pelvis/head/hands. Record contact velocities, toe/knee clearance, trajectory and landing pose. Fit to measured obstacle top/depth rather than assumed fixture dimensions; test capsule passage, overhead obstruction, destination floor, changed blockers and interruption. Resolve the planted hand's weapon attachment before activation. Preserve editable sources and CharacterMovement authority.

The next measurement pass can use an asset-only `-run=pythonscript -NullRHI` commandlet: evaluate the installed vault with `incorporate_root_motion_into_pose=True`, then its proposed owned derivative with `False`, without importing FBX or changing source assets. Bone samples alone cannot certify the full body or weapon sweep; final acceptance requires the actual gameplay view and recorded regressions in [TEST_MATRIX](../TEST_MATRIX.md).
