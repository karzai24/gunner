Official source: [Unreal Engine EULA](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/unreal), checked 2026-09-19. Section 1 defines content in installed Samples/Templates as Examples; section 5(b) permits distribution of Examples in source or object code. Retain Epic's ownership and applicable notices; do not relicense these as original Project Gunner content. The game's eventual credits/distribution must satisfy applicable Unreal notices. No new marketplace asset, purchase or license acceptance was performed in this task.

Seven UAL1 clips were retargeted: crouch idle, crouch forward, sprint, punch jab, punch cross, roll and sword attack. The locomotion composition selects **three** of these (crouch idle/forward and sprint). It additionally selects `Punch_Jab` for an unarmed melee montage and `Roll` for `AM_DodgeRoll`, a FullBody montage at rate scale 1.65 with native guarded movement. Cross and sword attack remain dormant candidates. Retarget output, script references and asset presence do not certify live animation quality. Record the actual build/play/visual results and any enablement changes in `TEST_MATRIX.md` and `ANIMATION_PIPELINE.md`.

No dedicated directional crouch, wall-cover pose library, knife-specific motion or through-vault animation was obtained from these free Standard sources. Marketplace alternatives and paid direct-creator tiers were researched only; none were purchased or imported. Do not redistribute marketplace content under the installed Examples allowance. Final Micah/Rook art remains deferred and must preserve original identity and avoid recognizable franchise designs.

The blind-fire extension creates `ABP_WardenBlindFire` using the same licensed crouch and armed clips. Native two-bone arm IK and sampled weapon-grip transforms are project-authored procedural composition; no source clip is relabeled as blind fire, no replacement motion tracks are keyed, and no additional marketplace assets are acquired. `Tools/install_blind_fire.py` retains recoverable backups before assigning the new graph and grip data.

The crouched-reload extension creates `ABP_WardenCrouchReload` by layering the existing Epic rifle/pistol reload arm branches over the existing Quaternius crouch. It retains blind fire and preserves both earlier graphs. No source clips are modified, no new keyed tracks are authored, and no additional assets or licenses are acquired. `Tools/install_crouch_reload.py` preserves the assigned character package before reassignment.
